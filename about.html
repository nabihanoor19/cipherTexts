Analyst conventions

Substitution
One fixed permutation maps plaintext A-Z to ciphertext A-Z. Case and punctuation survive.
Vigenere
A=0 through Z=25. Only letters consume key positions.
Rail Fence
The standard zigzag changes positions, not characters. Operational notes constrain the depth to 2-8.
One-time pad
Plaintext, key, and ciphertext are bytes. The supplied ciphertext equals plaintext XOR pad.

Evidence discipline

Save frequency counts, partial mappings, key calculations, every Rail Fence candidate, and the reconstructed pad order for your report. Encoding is not encryption; no Base64 step is used in this case.
